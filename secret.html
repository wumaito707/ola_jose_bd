SOMETHING I WANTED YOU TO KNOW

This Part Is Just For You.

Joseph, when I look back at everything, I realize that our friendship became part of my life so naturally that I didn't notice how meaningful it had become.

It started at Ayegun. We were just students sharing the same school, but those ordinary school days slowly became years of memories. The jokes, the people around us, the rabbits, the conversations and all the little moments became things I can now look back on and smile about.

Then came the days outside school—the camp, your church, Christmas, the nights we spent having fun, the New Year, the gift you gave me and the times we helped each other. None of those moments needed to be perfect to become important.

I just want you to know that I genuinely appreciate having you as my friend. Life keeps changing and taking people in different directions, but I hope whatever happens next, you will always remember that our friendship was one of the good parts of my story.

🔐

The Story You Have to Solve

I met you when I thought I was simply making a friend.

Little did I know that some people quietly become important without asking permission.

Over the years, the jokes, arguments, journeys, and ordinary days started meaning more than they should.

Very often, I found myself wanting to keep those moments forever.

Even now, I still can't explain why your presence feels different.

Yesterday, today, and tomorrow somehow all lead back to the same person.

One thing remains hidden behind every memory and every smile.

Until you solve what the beginning of this story is trying to tell you. ❤️🔐

Hint: Sometimes the first step of every sentence is more important than the story itself. 👀

Thank you for the laughter, the memories, the friendship and simply for being Joseph.

— Adewole ❤️
