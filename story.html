CHAPTER TWO

OUR STORY

Some people enter your life as classmates. Somehow, you become more than that.

THE BEGINNING

Where I Met You

I didn't know it at the time, but meeting you at Ayegun Baptist Church College would become one of those moments I would eventually look back on with gratitude. We were simply students sharing the same school, surrounded by teachers, classmates, jokes and everyday school life. But somewhere between those ordinary days, a friendship began to grow.

THE YEARS WE SHARED

Six Years. Countless Memories.

Six years at Ayegun gave us enough memories to fill more than one page. There were funny moments, serious moments, people we met and lessons we learned. There were days when everything felt easy and days when life wasn't quite as simple. Through all of it, you became someone I could call my friend.

When I think about those school years now, I don't remember only the classroom. I remember the people, the laughter, the little adventures and the moments that made those years feel like a chapter of our lives.

01

ONE OF THE SMALL MEMORIES

The Rabbit Story

One of the funny things I remember is how I ended up caring about rabbits partly because you were doing it too. It wasn't some huge life-changing event. It was just one of those small things that happened during school. But those small things are exactly what make memories feel real when you look back years later.

THE END OF SCHOOL

Then We Had To Leave

Eventually, six years came to an end and graduation arrived. Everyone began thinking about what would come next. Some people moved toward LAUTECH, while my own path eventually led me somewhere different.

Leaving school meant leaving behind a familiar place, familiar faces and a routine we had known for years. But leaving a place doesn't mean leaving every memory behind.

A NEW CHAPTER

Different Roads, Same Memories

Life started changing after Ayegun. New schools, new responsibilities and new experiences became part of our lives. I started my own journey at Emmanuel Alayande University, while you continued yours.

Things were no longer exactly the way they were in school, but the friendship didn't suddenly become meaningless. Sometimes growing up simply means learning how to carry old memories while creating new ones.

02

DECEMBER 2024

Four Days I'll Remember

December gave us another chapter when I went to camp with you and Marvellous. It was also my first time attending your church. There were long days, worship, food, laughter, tired nights and moments that were simply fun because we were together.

Even the imperfect moments became memories. That is what makes real friendship different. Everything doesn't have to go perfectly for a moment to become special.

IF I HAD TO SUM IT UP

I'm Glad Our Paths Crossed.

I could write about every joke, every conversation, every mistake and every good moment, but even then, I probably wouldn't capture everything our friendship has meant.

What I know is simple: meeting you became part of my story, and I'm genuinely grateful for that.
